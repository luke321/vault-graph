# What the graph showed about this vault

Structural diagnostics from a real run. These are **observations, not decisions** — they
age with the vault, and they are here because they are why several design choices exist.


Worth knowing before reading the disc. Remeasured 2026-08-21 on 443 notes /
1419 links:

- **Only 8.9% of links stay inside one PARA folder** (127 of 1419). `03 - Resources`
  <-> `08 - Meeting Notes` alone is 785 of them, then `04 - Daily Notes` <->
  `08 - Meeting Notes` at 152 and `03 - Resources` <-> `04 - Daily Notes` at 121. The
  folder grouping is a filing system, not the knowledge structure — which is why
  Core / edge and Community existed, and it is also the number that justifies the
  curved-edge toggle: 91% of links cross the disc.
- **Clean core-periphery.** 8 shells: a 34-note 8-core (people and projects) down to
  127 notes in the 1-core fringe.
- **A daily note is the #2 broker** by betweenness, above any project or person note.
  A dated journal entry holding the graph together is usually a hub by accident.
- **125 dead ends** (exactly one link), 0 orphans, 141 links to notes that do not exist.

The core-periphery and betweenness figures above are from the 2026-08-20 pass and
have not been recomputed — the k-core code was removed with the Core / edge view, so
they stand as a record rather than a live measurement. Everything else on this list
is current.
