Open questions · design gate

  1. D-1: should bundles be presentation or real groups? Recommend presentation: real tags keep colours, rows, pins and hide/only controls; the shared wedge gets a range label. Real groups require new colours, visibility and saved settings, expanding #118's scope.
  2. D-2: should membership stay fixed during filtering? Recommend stable membership from the unfiltered dimension; recut only on a data rebuild. It preserves findability but permits sparse filtered bundles. Recutting after every filter improves fill and moves tags between names.
